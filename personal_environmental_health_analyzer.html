Key trends & observations

Mathura's 2026 annual AQI (113 US) is 5.7% improved vs prior years but still far above WHO guidelines
Mathura groundwater TDS ranges 962–6,647 mg/L — avg 2,696 mg/L (5× safe limit)
86% of Mathura water samples exceeded BIS TDS standards; 82% exceeded hardness limits
Northern UP cities dominate worst AQI rankings globally — 6 of top 10 most polluted are in UP
Bengaluru's summer AQI (44) is better than Mathura's best recorded days — a 3× gap
Biggest anomaly: Mathura's water hardness reaches 6,250 mg/L in some borewells — over 34× the WHO guideline of 180 mg/L. This extreme mineral load has direct daily impacts on hair, skin, and scalp health for every resident.

Daily air quality actions

Wear N95/N99 mask outdoors — Mathura AQI 144 makes unprotected exposure equivalent to smoking 1.1 cigarettes/day
Avoid outdoor activity 10 AM–4 PM when PM2.5 peaks; best outdoor time is early morning (5–7 AM)
Keep car windows closed, use recirculated air while driving in heavy traffic
Check real-time AQI on IQAir or CPCB app before any outdoor plans

Indoor air improvements

Install HEPA air purifier (CADR ≥200 m³/hr) — removes 99.97% of PM2.5; target one per bedroom and living area
Add air-purifying plants: snake plant, peace lily, spider plant — improve indoor air naturallyxtra
Seal window gaps during high-AQI days; use door draft stoppers
Avoid indoor burning (incense, mosquito coils) — drastically raises indoor PM2.5

Exercise guidance

Move exercise indoors: yoga, home workouts, gym — avoid strenuous outdoor cardio when AQI >100
Plan outdoor runs/cycling for post-rain days — AQI typically drops 30–40% after rainfall

Water & hair care

Install RO+UV water purifier — essential given Mathura TDS avg 2,696 mg/L; target <150 mg/L for drinking
Install shower filter or use softened water for hair wash — hard water strips natural oils causing breakage & dryness
Rinse hair with diluted apple cider vinegar (1:3 ratio) weekly — neutralises mineral buildup from hard water
Use deep conditioning hair mask with coconut or argan oil twice weekly to combat pollution-induced dryness

Skin care

Double-cleanse at night to remove PM2.5 particles that penetrate pores — micellar water followed by gentle face wash
Apply antioxidant serum (Vitamin C) daily — neutralises free radicals from PM2.5 exposure
Use ceramide-based moisturiser — hard water strips skin barrier; ceramides repair it
Drink 2.5–3 L filtered water daily — combats skin dryness amplified by hard water bathing
